About Hawk-Dove & ESS

Hawks always escalate conflicts — they fight for resources and win them all, but risk serious injury fighting other Hawks. Doves display but retreat if escalation occurs — never injured, but lose resources to Hawks.

When V > C: Hawks always beat the cost of fighting, so Hawks dominate — pure Hawk population is the ESS.

When V < C: Fighting other Hawks is too costly. Neither pure Hawks nor pure Doves is stable. The ESS is a mixed population of V/C proportion Hawks.

Biological examples: Elephant seals (high V/C → fierce fights), Songbirds (moderate C → ritualized displays), Fiddler crabs (claw size signals — Dove-like assessment before escalation).
